Plumbing services

Specialist plumbing for homes and businesses.

Emergency Plumbing

A plumbing emergency does not wait for office hours, and neither do we. Our call-out teams are dispatched around the clock to stop the damage first, then fix the cause properly.

  • Isolate and stop the water quickly
  • Repair or replace burst and damaged pipes
  • Make geysers and connected electrics safe
  • Clear blockages and flooding
  • Provide a written report for insurance claims

Leak Detection and Repair

Hidden leaks waste water, damage structures and push up your bill. We use modern, non-invasive detection to pinpoint the source accurately, so repairs are targeted and disruption is minimal.

  • Pressure testing of the water system
  • Acoustic and thermal leak detection
  • Underground and under-slab leak tracing
  • Pool and garden line checks
  • Targeted repair with a written findings report

Drain Unblocking and Inspection

Slow or blocked drains are rarely a one-off. We clear the blockage, inspect the line and tell you plainly what caused it, so it does not keep coming back.

  • High-pressure drain jetting
  • Camera inspection of the pipe
  • Root and grease removal
  • Repair of collapsed or damaged pipes
  • Preventive maintenance plans

Geyser Installation and Repair

A faulty geyser is a leading cause of water damage at home and a large part of the electricity bill. We install, repair and replace electric, solar and heat pump systems with the right safety valves and drip trays.

  • Supply and fit electric, solar and heat pump systems
  • Replace valves, elements and thermostats
  • Install drip trays and safe overflow pipes
  • Fit pressure-control and vacuum-breaker valves
  • Issue a Certificate of Compliance where required

Bathroom and Kitchen Plumbing

A beautiful bathroom or kitchen is only as good as the plumbing you cannot see. We handle pipework, fixtures and fittings, working with your builder, designer and tiler.

  • First-fix and second-fix plumbing
  • Relocate basins, toilets, showers and sinks
  • Supply and fit fixtures and fittings
  • Replace ageing pipework
  • Coordinate with your tiler and electrician

Commercial and Facilities Plumbing

Businesses need plumbing partners who understand compliance, access and downtime. We provide planned maintenance, rapid call-outs and reporting for body corporates, facilities managers and corporate clients.

  • Planned preventative maintenance programmes
  • Rapid call-outs with job cards
  • Water meter and backflow installation
  • Tenant installations and refurbishments
  • Monthly reporting and compliance documents

Plumbing Certificate of Compliance

A Certificate of Compliance confirms that plumbing work meets the required standards. We inspect, fix any non-compliance and issue the certificate, so selling, renting or insuring your property is not held up.

  • Full inspection against the standards
  • A written list of any defects
  • Repair of non-compliant work
  • Issue of the certificate
  • Advice on what your property requires
